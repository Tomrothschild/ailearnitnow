# AI Learn It Now

A simple educational website that helps beginners understand practical uses for artificial intelligence.

## Mission

Make AI simple, useful, and approachable for everyday people—especially beginners, older adults, families, and small-business owners.

## Website Goal

1. Give visitors free, practical AI guidance.
2. Let visitors ask questions through a chatbot.
3. Collect interested leads.
4. Direct people who want structured education to The College of AI.
5. Allow qualified business prospects to book a 15-minute phone call.
